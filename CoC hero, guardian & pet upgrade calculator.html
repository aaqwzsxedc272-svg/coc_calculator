Base times: no Gold Pass, Books, Hammers or potions, and Hero Hall, Pet House and Town Hall requirements aren’t checked. Heroes and guardians each need a builder; pets upgrade in the Pet House, one at a time and without a builder. “All at once” is the longest hero or guardian upgrade or the whole pet queue, whichever is longer. Hammer Jam runs Nov 1 08:00 to Nov 16 08:00 UTC, 2026, and upgrades started during it keep the shorter time; the 50% is applied to guardians and pets too. Guardians (Smasher, Longshot, Logger) share one cost and time table. Diggy is listed to level 10 only: the August update added levels 11 to 15, but I couldn’t find verified per-level costs.
