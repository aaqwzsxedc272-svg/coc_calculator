Heroes, guardians & pets — plan, start, count down

Tick what you want to upgrade, set the level you have and your Town Hall, then press Start (▸) on an item. The app runs the real build timers in the background, keeps counting after you close the tab, and warns you 5 minutes before anything finishes.

Real game art is used for the icons (all rights belong to Supercell). Costs and times are the base values: no Gold Pass, Books, Hammers or potions, and Hero Hall / Pet House levels aren’t checked. Heroes and guardians share the same builder count (set it on the right); pets upgrade in the Pet House, one at a time, without a builder, and the queue starts from now (it isn’t retroactive). Hammer Jam runs Nov 1 08:00 → Nov 16 08:00 UTC 2026 and upgrades started during it keep the shorter time; the 50% also applies to guardians and pets. Guardians (Smasher, Longshot, Logger) share one cost/time table. Diggy is listed to level 10 only. Data: cocwarbase.com (heroes), Clash of Clans Wiki, cocmap.com and coclayout.com (guardians and pets), checked Nov 7, 2026.
